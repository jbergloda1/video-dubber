Privacy Policy — Video Dubber VN

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Video Dubber VN is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It translates, dubs and subtitles videos in Vietnamese and lets the owner send the finished videos to their own TikTok account.

What data the app uses

  • When you connect TikTok, the app receives from TikTok an access token, a refresh token and your open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), and the permission to upload videos (video.upload / video.publish).
  • The app never sees or stores your TikTok password: you sign in on TikTok's own page.

How the data is used and stored

  • Tokens are stored only on the computer where the app runs and are used only to upload videos you choose to post.
  • No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Deleting your data

Click "Đăng xuất" (Log out) in the app's settings to delete the stored tokens, or revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app owner through the TikTok account that uses this app.
